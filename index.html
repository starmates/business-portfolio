What does "turnkey" actually mean to someone buying interiors?

Chennai, India. Turnkey interiors. Website and SEO.

Mani Interiors builds offices, hospitals, banks and homes in Chennai, with one team for every trade. Buyers often don't know what "turnkey" includes, so the website had to make it simple.

This bakery is made up. The switches are real.

  • Words

    Nobody searches for "quality products and services". This says nothing a visitor can use.

  • Type

    Everything is the same size, so the eye has nowhere to start.

  • Layout and photo

    A small photo squeezed under the text. The bread is the sales pitch, and it is hiding.

  • Colour

    Pale grey on beige looks tired and is hard to read.

  • Next step

    Click where? A small link that names nothing gets ignored.
